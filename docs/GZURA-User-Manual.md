# GZURA — complete user manual

**Feature status:** see [GZURA-Feature-Status-Report.md](./GZURA-Feature-Status-Report.md) for what is built, what is missing, and what differs from the additional website requirements you shared.

This is the full guide for people who use the website. It walks you from your first visit through creating an account, joining an event, paying, using your digital pass, applying to host a course, and (for staff) approving events and checking people in.

## What you can do on the website

- **Browse** published events and courses: date, time, venue, price, and remaining seats when a limit is set.
- **Create an account** with email, Google, or phone OTP.
- **Join** a free event, or **pay** for a paid event through Razorpay (cards, UPI, and net banking as enabled on the payment screen).
- **Open your digital pass** (QR code) for offline events, and **join Google Meet** for online events after you are enrolled.
- **Read materials** the host uploaded, and **download invoices** for paid enrollments.
- **Apply to host** a course. After staff approve you, you can create events that stay private until they are reviewed.
- **Staff** can approve events and host applications, manage users, and scan passes at the door.

## Chapter 1. Your first visit

### 1.1 Open the home page

The home page introduces GZURA. From here you can:

- Read about the community and programmes
- Open **Events** and upcoming sessions
- Read success stories and testimonials
- **Join** the community or create an account
- Apply to **host a course** (form on the home page)
- Open **Contact**, **FAQ**, **About**, **Privacy**, and **Terms**

### 1.2 Top menu

Typical items:

- **About**
- **Programs**
- **Events**
- **Join**
- **Contact**
- **Login** / **Sign up** (when you are not signed in)

On a phone, open the menu icon to see the same links.

### 1.3 After you sign in

**Members** land on the member home, with events you can join and a menu for **My Learnings**, **Invoices**, and **Profile**.

**Hosts** and **administrators** land on the admin dashboard. Hosts see **Dashboard** and **Events**. Administrators also see **Host Requests** and **Users**.

### 1.4 Policies and refunds

Open **Terms**, **Privacy**, **Cookies**, and **FAQ** from the footer. Cancellation and refund wording is in **Terms** and **FAQ**. Read them before you pay. The payment popup itself does not always repeat the full policy.

## Chapter 2. Create your account

You need an account to join an event or pay. You can still browse public pages without an account.

### 2.1 Register as a member

1. Click **Sign up**.
2. Choose **email and password**, **Google**, or **phone OTP** (when that option is shown).
3. Complete the form and confirm.
4. You are signed in. You may see **Onboarding** to pick goals and interests. Finish that screen, then continue.

If you already have an account, use **Login** instead.

### 2.2 Finish onboarding (first login)

When you see onboarding:

1. Choose your goals and interests.
2. Save.

You can update details later under **Profile**.

### 2.3 Sign in

1. Click **Login**.
2. Sign in with the same method you used to register.
3. After login:
   - Members go to the member home.
   - Hosts and administrators go to the admin dashboard.

### 2.4 Forgot password

1. Open **Forgot password** from the login page.
2. Enter your email.
3. Open the reset link in your inbox and set a new password.

If you do not receive the email, check spam, or contact GZURA staff so they can confirm email is working on the server.

### 2.5 Sign out

Open your name or photo menu and choose **Sign out**.

### 2.6 Roles in one line

| Role | After login | What you can do |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Member** | Member home | Join events, pay, pass, learnings, invoices, profile |
| **Host** | Admin dashboard | Create and manage your own events; see people enrolled in those events |
| **Administrator** | Admin dashboard | Approve events and hosts, manage users, check people in at the door |

## Chapter 3. Find an event

This chapter is the path from the public list to the event page.

### 3.1 Where events come from

Only **published, approved** events appear for visitors. A host who just submitted an event is **Pending**. Visitors do not see it until staff approve it.

### 3.2 Browse from the public site

1. Open **Events**.
2. Browse the cards. You typically see a photo, title, date, and price (or Free).
3. Click a card to open the full page.

You can also open upcoming events from the home page.

### 3.3 Browse after you sign in (members)

1. Sign in.
2. Open the member home.
3. Open an event the same way. The join and pay buttons are on this page.

### 3.4 Read an event page

You should see:

- Title, description, and photos
- **Date** and **time**
- **Online** or **Offline**
- **Venue** or location (and a map when the organiser added one)
- **Price** in rupees. If a **member price** is lower, that is shown as well
- **Seats remaining** when the organiser set a maximum

For online events, meeting details appear after you enrol (see [Chapter 5](#chapter-5-after-you-join)).

## Chapter 4. Join an event

You must be **signed in** to join or pay.

### 4.1 Join a free event

1. Open the event page.
2. If you are not signed in, click join. The site takes you to **Login**. After login you return to the event.
3. Click **Join**.
4. Wait until the confirmation appears. The event is added to **My Learnings**.
5. Check your email for confirmation and a **View Digital Pass** link (when email is working).

If you are **already enrolled**, the site will not create a second booking for the same email or account.

If the event is **full**, join is refused. Look for another date or wait for a new session.

### 4.2 Pay for a paid event

1. Sign in as a member.
2. Open the paid event.
3. Click the pay / join button.
4. Complete payment in the **Razorpay** window (UPI, card, or net banking, depending on what Razorpay shows).
5. Do not close the window until you see success on GZURA.
6. You are enrolled. You may also receive an invoice email.

**Member price:** if the event has a lower member price, you are charged that amount when you are signed in as a member.

**What is not available today**

- Paying by **manual UPI** outside Razorpay and waiting for staff to verify a screenshot.
- Paying as a **guest** without an account.

Use the **event page** for paid events. There is an older **register** page for some free flows; it is not the main paid checkout.

### 4.3 If payment fails

Stay on the event page and try again. If money left your account but you have no enrollment, keep the Razorpay payment id and contact GZURA staff. Do not assume you are enrolled until the site shows success.

## Chapter 5. After you join

### 5.1 Digital pass and QR code

1. Open the **View Digital Pass** link in your email, or open the pass from the event page after you enrol.
2. You see your name, the event, and a **QR code**.
3. At an **offline** venue, show this screen (or a print) at the door.

Keep the link private. Anyone with the link can display the same pass.

### 5.2 Online events — Google Meet

1. Enrol first (free join or paid join).
2. Open **Join meeting** (or the Meet page for that event) when it is time.
3. The site checks that you are enrolled, then shows the Google Meet link.

If there is no meeting link, the host has not added it yet. Contact them or GZURA staff.

### 5.3 My Learnings and course materials

1. Open **My Learnings**.
2. Open the event you joined.
3. If the host uploaded notes or files, open **course content** and download or read them.

### 5.4 Invoices

1. Open **Invoices**.
2. Find the paid event.
3. **Download** the receipt.

Free events do not create a paid invoice.

### 5.5 Profile

Open **Profile** to update your name, phone, photo, and related details. You can link a phone with OTP when that option is shown.

## Chapter 6. Apply to host a course

Use this if you want to teach on GZURA. This is **not** the same as joining an event as a participant.

### 6.1 The form on the home page (the one that is saved)

1. Open the home page and scroll to the **host a course** section.
2. Enter your **name**, **email**, **phone**, **gender**, **profession**, **interest**, preferred **date** and **time**, and a **message** about the course.
3. Submit.

You should see a success message. Staff receive the application. You may also get an email that the application was received.

Staff review it under **Host Requests**. When they **Approve**, your account can work as a **Host**.

### 6.2 The Join page form (membership interest)

The **Join** page has a community form (name, city, interests, how you heard about GZURA, terms). Submitting it takes you to **Sign up** with your name and email filled in. It does **not** by itself create a host application. To apply as a trainer, use the home-page host form in 6.1.

### 6.3 Contact form

**Contact** is for general questions. Staff receive the message in the system. There is no public “ticket number” page for you to track it.

## Chapter 7. For hosts

Hosts sign in at **Login** and land on the admin area. Your menu is **Dashboard** and **Events** only. You do not see **Users** or **Host Requests**. That is expected.

### 7.1 Create an event or course

1. Open **Events**.
2. Click **New** (or the equivalent add button).
3. Fill in:
   - Title and description
   - **Online** or **Offline**
   - Date, time, and location (or map pin)
   - Photos
   - Price in rupees, optional member price, and maximum seats
   - Speaker name and bio, course outline
   - For online: Google Meet fields when they are shown
4. Save as **Draft**, or submit for approval.

A host-created event stays **Pending** until an administrator approves it. Visitors do not see it until then.

### 7.2 After you submit

| What you see | What visitors see | What you should do |
|--------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| **Draft** | Nothing | Finish the form and submit |
| **Pending** | Nothing | Wait for review |
| **Published** | The public event page | Share the link; watch enrollments |
| **Rejected** | Nothing | Read the reason in the **activity log**, edit, and **Resubmit** |

If you change a **published** event, some edits wait for staff approval so the live page does not change until they agree.

### 7.3 See who enrolled

1. Open **Events** and open your event.
2. Open **Enrolled users**.
3. Search by name, email, phone, city, or profession.
4. Check whether payment is **Free** or **Paid**.

This list is **not** the same as **Host Requests**. Host Requests is only for trainer applications, and only administrators see it.

### 7.4 Course content

On the same event, open **Course content**. Add text or files. Enrolled members see them under **My Learnings**.

### 7.5 If the event is rejected

Open the **activity log**, read the notes, update the event, and **Resubmit**.

## Chapter 8. For administrators

Full menu: **Dashboard**, **Events**, **Host Requests**, **Users**.

**Important name:** **Host Requests** is trainer applications. Event participants are under **Events → (the event) → Enrolled users**.

### 8.1 Dashboard

Open the admin home. You see counts, charts, recent activity, and top events. Use this as a snapshot, not as a full finance report.

### 8.2 Approve or reject events

1. Open **Events**.
2. Filter by **Pending** (or **Rejected**) if you need a short list.
3. Open the event. Read the details.
4. **Approve** to publish, or **Reject** and give a reason.
5. If a host edited a live event, review **pending changes** the same way.

You can also feature an event, edit any field, or delete when that is required.

### 8.3 Host requests

1. Open **Host Requests**.
2. Search or filter **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.
3. Open a row to read the application.
4. **Approve** — the person can work as a host (a host user is created or linked).
5. **Reject** if the application is not a fit.
6. You can update several rows at once with bulk actions.
7. **Export** the current list to Excel, Word, or PDF. This export is the **host applications**, not a full attendee finance report.

### 8.4 Users

1. Open **Users**.
2. Create a user, change the **role** (Member, Host, Admin), or **block** an account.
3. Edit profile details when needed.

### 8.5 Check-in at the door (offline)

The scanner is **not** in the side menu. Bookmark this page:

**https://www.gzura.com/admin/qr-scanner**

1. Sign in as an **administrator**.
2. Open the scanner page and allow the camera.
3. Scan the QR on the attendee’s pass (or paste the pass link).
4. You should see a valid result. The person is marked checked in.

Rules today:

- This is for **offline** events.
- Scanning the same pass again does not create a second check-in time. The first time is kept. You may still see a success message, so treat a repeat scan as “already in”.

### 8.6 Notifications

While you are signed in, the bell can show pending host requests and events waiting for approval.

You may also get **email** when someone applies as a host or submits an event (if email is configured). You do **not** get an email for every new event registration today.

## Chapter 9. If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to try |
|--------------|-------------|
| Cannot join a paid event | Sign in first. Use the main event page, not an old register link. Check that the event is not full. |
| Payment window closed too early | Open the event again. If you were charged, contact staff with the payment id. |
| No confirmation email | Check spam. Ask staff to confirm email sending. You can still open **My Learnings** and the pass from the site. |
| Pass says invalid | Use the exact link from the email or from your enrollment. Do not type part of the address by hand. |
| No Meet link | You must be enrolled. The host must have added a meeting link. |
| Host cannot see Users | Expected. Only administrators see Users and Host Requests. |
| Door scanner not in the menu | Open `/admin/qr-scanner` while signed in as admin. |
